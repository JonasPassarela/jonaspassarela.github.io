1 Executive Summary & Local-First Architecture

Magic Deck Master (package ID: com.jpassarela.magicdeckmaster) is engineered with a strict local-first architecture. We believe that your Magic: The Gathering card collection, customized decks, wishlist, and usage habits are strictly personal.

Core Commitments:

  • No Account Required: You do not need to register, provide an email, or log in to use the core features of the app.
  • No Analytics or Tracking SDKs: We do not track your clicks, sessions, geolocation, or hardware identifiers.
  • Zero Cloud Databases: We do not operate proprietary user servers that hold your personal inventory.

2 Camera Permission (On-Device OCR Optical Card Scanner)

The app requests access to the device camera strictly when you tap the Card Scanner feature. This permission is governed by the following technical guarantees:

100% Offline / Local Processing (WASM)

Optical Character Recognition (OCR) is executed locally on your device hardware using an in-app WebAssembly (WASM) engine (Tesseract.js). Frames are processed in volatile memory and never transmitted to any external server.

No Video or Photo Storage

Camera video frames are immediately discarded from memory once the text is identified. The app does not save photos to your photo gallery, does not record audio or video, and does not perform any biometric or facial scanning.

Camera permission can be enabled or disabled at any time in your Android System Settings > Apps > Magic Deck Master > Permissions.

3 Google Drive API & OAuth 2.0 User Data Compliance

Magic Deck Master offers an optional, user-initiated cloud backup feature through the official Google Drive API.

Restricted Scope: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file

We intentionally use the most restrictive Google Drive scope available: drive.file. This scope grants the app permission solely and exclusively to create, view, and modify files that were created by Magic Deck Master itself (e.g., your collection backup file named magic-deck-master-backup.json).

  • The app cannot read, browse, modify, or delete any other files or folders in your Google Drive account.
  • OAuth authentication tokens are held only in temporary client-side session memory and are never transmitted to or stored on third-party servers.
  • Our use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adhere strictly to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

You can revoke Magic Deck Master's access to your Google Drive at any time via your Google Account Security Dashboard at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

4 Local Device Storage (IndexedDB & localStorage)

All your decks, inventory quantities, price watches, and personal tags are stored entirely within your device's local application storage sandbox (using standard browser / WebView IndexedDB and localStorage APIs).

This data remains on your physical phone. Clearing the app storage or uninstalling the application via Android settings will permanently erase all local records unless you performed a manual export or backup.

5 Third-Party APIs & Data Providers

To provide real-time card search, rulings, printings, and Brazilian market price estimates, the app communicates directly with public third-party APIs via HTTPS:

  • Scryfall API (scryfall.com): Used to fetch public Magic: The Gathering card catalog data, Oracle texts, legalities, and card image assets. Queries are read-only and send no user PII.
  • LigaMagic (ligamagic.com.br): Used for fetching Brazilian Real (BRL) market pricing benchmarks. Queries are anonymous and read-only.

6 Advertising, Tracking & Commercial Data Sales

Zero Advertising Policy

  • Magic Deck Master does not display third-party advertisements or banners.
  • The app does not collect, access, or transmit the Google Advertising ID (GAID / AAID).
  • We do not sell, rent, monetize, or broker any user data to third parties, data aggregators, or marketing networks.

8 Data Deletion Instructions & Support Contact

In accordance with Google Play User Data policies, users have full autonomy over the deletion of any data associated with Magic Deck Master:

A. Local Device Data Deletion

Go to Android Settings > Apps > Magic Deck Master > Storage > Clear Storage / Clear Data, or simply uninstall the application. All local databases, cached card images, and preferences are purged immediately and permanently.

B. Cloud Backup Data Deletion

Open your Google Drive app or visit drive.google.com, locate the magic-deck-master-backup.json file, and delete it. You may also revoke the application permission in your Google Account settings.

C. Direct Privacy Inquiries & Rights Requests
